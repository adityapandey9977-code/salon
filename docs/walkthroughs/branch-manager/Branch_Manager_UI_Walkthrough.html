Branch Manager Workspace

Top Navigation Architecture, Front-Desk Operations & Comprehensive PRD Specification

Executive Summary

The Branch Manager / Reception panel has been updated from a fixed vertical sidebar to a unified Top Navbar Layout identical in sophistication to the Admin panel. Designed strictly around the dedicated single-branch manager persona (Indrapuri Flagship Outlet · Bhopal), the architecture consolidates brand identity and assigned location into a clean single column, streams front-desk diary operations into focused dropdowns, and eliminates unnecessary multi-branch selector bloat while maintaining 100% PRD compliance and design system reusability.

1. UI Architecture & Page Flow

Brand & Location (Single Column)
Combines Atelier Brand Logo with the active assigned outlet Indrapuri Flagship · Bhopal. Eliminates cross-location selector dropdowns since branch managers are strictly scoped to one branch.
Front Desk Operations
Integrated dropdown covering Appointments Diary (multi-service calendar), Walk-ins & Live Queue (wait time tokens), and Client Health & Allergy Cautions (ammonia/latex alerts).
Clients & Team Management
Direct access to Client CRM & Household Profiles, Stylist Rosters & Attendance, and Branch Service Menu with treatment buffers and station allocations.
POS, Commercial & Shift Closure
Covers New POS Checkout (instant cart billing), Invoices & Receipts, Retail Shelf Stock (low-stock alerts), and Daily Cashier Shift Closure with drawer cash balancing.

3. Business Rationale & Why We Implemented Everything

Why Single Column Brand & Branch Identifier?
Unlike the Brand Owner / Super Director (who oversees 5+ multi-city branches), a Branch General Manager is dedicated to their specific physical outlet (Indrapuri Flagship). Showing a multi-location switcher dropdown was not only redundant but confused operational accountability. Unifying the brand and outlet into a single left-hand column frees up over 200px of header space and guarantees that shift badges, notifications, and profile controls never overflow on standard 1366px laptop screens.
Why Top Navbar over Sidebar for Front Desk?
Front desk receptionists and branch managers spend 90% of their time looking at wide horizontal data structures: multi-column appointment diaries, live walk-in queue tables, and complex POS line items. A vertical sidebar permanently consumed 260px of screen real estate. The sticky Top Navbar maximizes the horizontal grid for appointment scheduling and allows rapid tab switching without vertical distraction.
Why Prominent Shift & Cashier Lock Controls?
In salon operations, cash leakage and untracked drawer discrepancies during shift handovers are major operational risks. Having the active shift badge (Shift #01 Active) directly in the header and an instant Close Cashier Shift action guarantees that drawer reconciliation happens on schedule before shift handover.
