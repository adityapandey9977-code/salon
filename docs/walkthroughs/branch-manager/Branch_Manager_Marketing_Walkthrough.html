2. Business Rationale & Why We Implemented Everything

1. Why Centralized Campaign & Promotion Governance is Necessary

In multi-outlet salon brands, rogue branch discounts severely erode gross margins. By centralizing Coupon Codes (e.g. GLOW20, WELCOME500) and Marketing Campaigns at the Head Office level, brand owners ensure that discount limits (e.g., max 2,000 uses) and minimum bill thresholds (e.g., Min Spend ₹3,500) are strictly validated at the POS across all outlets.

2. Why Multi-Tiered Loyalty Programs Drive High Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

Loyal salon clients account for 64.5% of total brand revenue (₹18.4L). The 4-tier loyalty architecture (Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond Elite) creates a gamified incentive for clients to increase visit frequency and basket size to earn up to 3x points multipliers and complimentary spa upgrades.

3. Why Branch-Wise Marketing Performance Comparison is Crucial

Not all promotional themes resonate equally across urban clusters. The Branch-Wise Performance Matrix reveals that while Indore Central yields high conversion on Keratin Hair Spa campaigns (₹9.8L), boutique outlets in Bhopal and Ujjain see greater lift from First-Visit Welcome Vouchers, allowing brand leadership to tailor marketing spend with precision.

4. Why Marketing Attribution Reports are Built In

Salon marketing budgets must prove measurable return on investment. The Attributed Revenue Engine (ROI 4.8x–6.1x) ties every invoice generated at checkout directly back to the active campaign or redeemed coupon code, providing leadership with irrefutable proof of marketing effectiveness.

3. UI Architecture & Page Flow

  • Master Controller (MarketingMasterPage.tsx): Hosts 5 sub-navigation tabs, breadcrumbs, and reactive URL query param synchronization (?tab=...).
  • Overview Tab (MarketingOverviewTab.tsx): 7 KPI metric cards, top campaign performance cards, offer type breakdown bars, new vs. returning client split, and branch comparison table.
  • Campaigns Tab (CampaignsTab.tsx): Master campaign register with lifecycle states, Create/Edit Campaign modal with live preview, and Campaign Details inspection modal.
  • Promotions & Offers (PromotionsOffersTab.tsx): Special offers register with coupon code tags, usage pacing, Create/Edit Offer modal, and branch utilization breakdown.
  • Loyalty Tab (LoyaltyTab.tsx): 5 Loyalty KPIs, enrolled member directory, 4-tier configuration matrix, and Member Loyalty Dossier modal with points timeline.
  • Marketing Reports (MarketingReportsTab.tsx): 4 report categories, live preview canvas with right-aligned numeric metrics, and CSV/Excel/PDF export triggers.
