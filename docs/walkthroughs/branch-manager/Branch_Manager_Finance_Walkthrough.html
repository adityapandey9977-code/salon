2. Business Rationale & Why We Implemented Everything

A. Multi-Branch Real-Time Scoping & RBAC Perspectives

In high-growth luxury salon chains, financial opacity between individual outlets and the corporate treasury leads to revenue leakage and unverified cashier shortfalls. By implementing FinanceBranchContext.tsx, corporate CFOs can toggle between consolidated chain views (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Bhiwandi) and branch-specific sub-ledgers. Role-based perspectives (Corporate CFO, Branch Accountant, HR & Payroll Lead, Finance Manager) ensure that staff only access authorized data.

B. GST SAC 999721 & HSN 3305 Compliance

Indian tax laws enforce a strict distinction between salon grooming services (SAC 999721 at 18% GST: CGST 9% + SGST 9% or IGST 18%) and retail cosmetic product sales (HSN 3305 / 3304). The customer receipt ledger explicitly isolates these tax components and records B2B client GSTIN numbers, enabling seamless GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filings without manual accounting reconciliations.

C. Cash Drawer Control, Shift Closeout & Z-Report Sign-off

Salon cash drawers are vulnerable to cash shrinkage during shift handovers. Our daily register architecture mandates opening float logging (₹5,000), petty cash vouchers with authorization sign-offs, and physical cash hand-count comparisons against book revenue. Any variance requires mandatory explanation before the drawer is locked and Z-Report posted.

D. Graduated Stylist Commission Slabs & Backbar Chemical Deductions

Top colorists and stylists generate significant service volume but also consume expensive chemical products (Olaplex, L'Oréal, Schwarzkopf). Our tiered commission engine calculates progressive slabs (5% under ₹50k, 10% between ₹50k-₹1L, 15% over ₹1L) on net service sales after deducting backbar chemical material costs, plus a 12% retail upsell bonus and 100% direct tip pass-through.

E. Statutory Payroll Engine & Corporate Bank Direct Deposit

Indian labor regulations mandate strict Provident Fund (PF 12% of basic), Employee State Insurance (ESI 0.75% of gross), State Professional Tax (PT ₹200/mo), and Income Tax TDS (Section 192). The payroll engine computes all 10 standard components from biometric attendance logs and generates bank-ready CMS NEFT direct deposit batch files for HDFC, ICICI, and Kotak Mahindra Bank.
