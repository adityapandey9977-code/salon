Head Office & Branch Manager Inventory Architecture

Module: Brand Owner / Head Office → Inventory • Version: 2.0 • Scope: Multi-Branch Stock, Service BOM, Transfers, Consumption & Procurement

Executive Summary & Architectural Context: The Salon SaaS platform maintains strict separation of concerns between the Brand Owner Inventory Desk and the separate Inventory / Procurement Operations Panel. While day-to-day store clerks perform physical receiving and stockroom picking in their operational desk, the Brand Owner interface serves as the centralized governance cockpit—overseeing multi-branch stock valuation, Service Recipe BOM compliance, Maker-Checker PO authorizations, inter-branch balancing transfers, chairside consumption variance leakage, and physical stocktake adjustments.

2. Business Rationale & Why We Implemented Everything

1. Why Centralized Stock Valuation is Critical for Brand Owners

In multi-branch salon networks, inventory represents the second largest current asset after cash. The FIFO Valuation Engine computes stock worth across all 5 branches (₹28,50,000), giving leadership immediate visibility into capital locked in backwash chemicals and retail displays without waiting for end-of-month accounting books.

2. Why Service Recipe / BOM (Bill of Materials) is Enforced

Without standardized recipes, colour mixing and treatment applications vary wildly by stylist. By enforcing Service BOMs (e.g. 50ml Majirel + 75ml Developer), the POS automatically calculates expected stock reduction upon service completion. When a stylist dispenses 75ml instead of 60ml (+25% variance), the system flags the leakage instantly, enabling targeted training rather than absorbing unbilled product loss.

3. Why Inter-Branch Stock Transfers Prevent Dead Stock

Slow-moving inventory in smaller branches (e.g., Ujjain or Bhopal) often expires before utilization. The Pre-Expiry Risk Matrix alerts Head Office 30 days prior, allowing one-click authorization of an Inter-Branch Transfer to high-volume locations like Indore Central, ensuring 100% stock utilization and zero chemical write-off.

4. Why Maker-Checker Purchase Order Approval is Mandatory

To eliminate unauthorized store purchases, storekeepers submit PO requisitions that require Head Office Brand Owner Sign-Off before vendor dispatch. Once delivered, Goods Receipt Notes (GRN) verify physical counts before sending summary notices to the separate Finance Panel for bank disbursement.

3. UI Architecture & Page Flow

  • Master Layout (InventoryMasterPage.tsx): Hosts 8 sub-navigation tabs, breadcrumb context, and reactive search param synchronizer.
  • Overview Tab (InventoryOverviewTab.tsx): 10 KPI summary cards, interactive shelf pacing distribution bar, and branch comparison table with multi-column sorting.
  • Products & Consumables (ProductsConsumablesTab.tsx): Retail catalogue, professional consumables, Product Dossier modal with batch inventory, and Service Recipe BOM interface.
  • Stock Tab (StockTab.tsx): Central stock ledger, running movement journal with delta badges, batch expiration monitor, and stock ageing analysis (0–30d to 180+d).
  • Procurement Tab (ProcurementTab.tsx): PO register, Vendor directory, GRN inward logs, Purchase Returns, and Finance Panel integration hooks.
  • Transfers Tab (TransfersTab.tsx): Inter-branch stock transfer ledger with 5-stage chain-of-custody progress tracker.
  • Consumption & Wastage (ConsumptionWastageTab.tsx): Backwash consumption ledger, Recipe vs. Actual variance analytics, Wastage approvals, and Retail returns.
  • Stocktake Tab (StocktakeTab.tsx): Physical cycle count ledger with line-by-line discrepancy reconciliation drawer.
  • Reports & Audit (InventoryReportsTab.tsx): 6 report categories, multi-format export (CSV/Excel/PDF), 4-severity risk alerts, and immutable audit journal.
