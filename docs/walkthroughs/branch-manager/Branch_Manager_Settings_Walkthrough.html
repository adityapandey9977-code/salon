2. Business Rationale & Why We Implemented Everything

A detailed rationale explaining the commercial need and architectural choices for each capability:

1. Why Centralized Brand Defaults?

Multi-unit salon enterprises operating across Indore, Bhopal, Ujjain, and Gwalior require consistent localization (Asia/Kolkata IST, Indian Lakhs/Crores, DD/MM/YYYY) without manual re-entry at each salon branch.

2. Why Dual Pricing Strategies & Deviation Matrix?

High-rent Flagship lounges (Vijay Nagar) command premium pricing (+15%), while Tier-2 express studios offer value rates. The deviation matrix allows leadership to audit checkout pricing variances in real time.

3. Why Cashier Discount Thresholds & Manager OTP?

Uncontrolled discounts are the #1 source of salon margin leakage. Restricting cashiers to 10% and requiring manager authorization for >15% prevents unapproved concessions at checkout.

4. Why 8 Explicit PRD Business Policies with Version Timelines?

Transparent policies for client consent (patch testing), chemical re-dos, package expirations, and no-shows prevent client disputes and ensure regulatory legal compliance across all franchises.

5. Why 9 Roles × 13 Modules Granular RBAC Matrix?

Front desk receptionists should book appointments and take payments but must not view payroll, edit base pricing, or export sensitive client phone rosters.

6. Why Non-Destructive Workflow Hiding?

When a tenant disables a module (e.g. Home Services or Loyalty), the PRD prohibits deleting historical invoices or records. The soft-hiding toggle hides UI surfaces while preserving historical financial integrity.

3. UI Architecture & Page Navigation Flow

The Brand Settings module is structured under a unified responsive master container with persistent sub-navigation:

  • Brand Profile (/brand-settings?tab=profile): Master corporate entity details, visual color tokens, and statutory tax registrations.
  • Locations & Defaults (/brand-settings?tab=locations): Regional localization settings and multi-branch configuration override modal.
  • Pricing & Tax (/brand-settings?tab=pricing): Pricing architecture, GST 18% rules, statutory SAC mapping, and cashier discount limits.
  • Policies (/brand-settings?tab=policies): 8 business policies register and policy dossier modal with full version history timeline.
  • Working Hours & Holidays (/brand-settings?tab=hours): 7-day shift editor, lunch break windows, and holiday calendar scheduler.
  • Roles & Permissions (/brand-settings?tab=roles): 9 system roles selector, 13 modules × 6 permissions interactive matrix, and role clone tool.
  • Brand Controls (/brand-settings?tab=controls): Module toggles with non-destructive workflow soft-hiding mechanisms.
  • Change History (/brand-settings?tab=audit): Immutable configuration change log auditing pricing, tax, and permission updates.
