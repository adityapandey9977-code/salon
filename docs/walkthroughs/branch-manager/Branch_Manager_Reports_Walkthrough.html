2. Business Rationale & Why We Implemented Everything

A detailed rationale explaining the commercial need and architectural choices for each capability:

1. Why Centralized Analytics without Operational Workflows?

Operational execution (editing shifts, managing inventory, booking appointments) belongs in specialized modules. Centralized Analytics gives the Brand Owner a holistic 360° overview across all 6 branches without operational clutter.

2. Why Specific PRD Revenue Stream Measures?

Salon businesses operate on blended revenue streams. Separating in-salon services (68.8%) from retail products (12.9%), package advances (10.4%), and recurring memberships (7.4%) gives leadership actionable cashflow visibility.

3. Why Recipe vs Actual Yield Variance Ledger?

Chemical over-dispensing (hair color, developer, facial serums) causes direct margin leakage. Tracking standard recipe doses against actual bowl dispensing enforces stylist discipline and reduces wastage to <0.4%.

4. Why Cohort Segmentation with Churn Risk?

Customer retention is 5x more cost-effective than new acquisition. Identifying 1,240 dormant clients (60-90 days lapsed) triggers targeted win-back campaigns before they become permanent churn risks.

5. Why Multi-Format Universal Export Engine?

Different executive stakeholders require specific formats: finance teams require raw CSV/Excel for reconciliation, while board members and auditors need styled executive PDF dossiers.

6. Why Lightweight Custom Report Builder?

Eliminates the complexity of heavy external BI tools while giving Brand Owners instant flexibility to mix modules (Revenue, Staff, Inventory) with primary grouping dimensions (Branch, Date, Category).

3. UI Architecture & Page Navigation Flow

The Reports & Analytics module is structured under a unified responsive master container with persistent sub-navigation:

  • Executive Overview (/reports?tab=overview): 8 comparative KPI cards, monthly revenue velocity bars, client ratio widgets, and branch ranking.
  • Operations (/reports?tab=operations): 7 appointment KPIs, weekly slot occupancy pacing, and origin channel distribution (App 48%, Call Center 22%).
  • Revenue (/reports?tab=revenue): 9 PRD revenue measures, service category split, payment tender methods, and multi-branch revenue matrix.
  • Staff Performance (/reports?tab=staff): Stylist productivity scorecard, workstation utilisation, rebooking rates, and commission accruals.
  • Client & Retention (/reports?tab=clients): 5 client cohort segments, visit frequency rhythm, and churn risk alerts.
  • Inventory (/reports?tab=inventory): Stock valuation, consumable usage, wastage discard, and recipe vs actual yield matrix.
  • Marketing (/reports?tab=marketing): Campaign reach, direct bookings captured, attendance rates, and ROI multipliers.
  • Franchise (/reports?tab=franchise): Licensed partner performance scorecard, chair utilisation, and 10% royalty pool tracking.
  • Custom Reports (/reports?tab=custom): Sub-tab toggle between the Custom Report Builder and the Saved Reports Library.
