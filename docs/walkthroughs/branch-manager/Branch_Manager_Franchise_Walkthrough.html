2. Business Rationale & Why We Implemented Everything

A detailed rationale explaining the commercial need and architectural choices for each capability:

1. Why Centralized Overview with 8 Core KPIs?

Brand Owners managing 18 franchise outlets across 6 cities need immediate visibility into network velocity, active entity health, and brand royalty accrual without logging into individual partner portals.

2. Why Dual-Section Partner Registration Form?

Separating partner basic identity (contact lead, email, phone) from commercial terms (authorized outlets, royalty model, agreement dates) ensures clean enterprise entity creation and contract compliance.

3. Why Independent Franchise Locations Directory?

Multi-unit partners (e.g. Apex Wellness LLP) operate 3 distinct outlets. Having a dedicated location registry allows individual branch footfall, manager tracking, and revenue benchmarking.

4. Why Dedicated Statutory Compliance Matrix?

Operating a salon brand requires stringent adherence to municipal trade licenses, Fire NOCs, and public liability policies. Lapses present existential brand risk; automated tracking prevents compliance default.

5. Why Read-Oriented Royalty Ledger with Finance Links?

Prevents redundant dual-accounting systems. Head Office monitors brand fee computations in Franchise, while actual bank disbursements and GST filing remain in the specialized Finance Panel.

6. Why 4 Distinct Report Categories with Live Canvas?

Executive stakeholders need tailored views: legal teams require Agreement Audits, operations require Location Growth, and CFOs require Partner-wise Royalty statements in CSV/Excel/PDF.

3. UI Architecture & Page Navigation Flow

The Franchise module is structured under a unified responsive master container with persistent sub-navigation:

  • Franchise Overview (/franchise?tab=overview): 8 KPI metric cards, regional revenue split, top partner leaderboard, and governance alert cards.
  • Franchise Partners (/franchise?tab=partners): Entity registry, search & region filters, Create Partner modal, and Partner Profile Dossier.
  • Franchise Locations (/franchise?tab=locations): Multi-branch directory, branch manager contacts, and Outlet Dossier with service performance.
  • Agreements & Compliance (/franchise?tab=agreements): Sub-tab toggle between Legal Contracts Ledger and Statutory Compliance Matrix.
  • Commissions & Royalties (/franchise?tab=royalties): 5 financial KPIs, billing voucher ledger, and math computation dossier modal.
  • Franchise Reports (/franchise?tab=reports): Category selector (Partner, Location, Agreement, Commission), parameter customizers, and live table preview.
