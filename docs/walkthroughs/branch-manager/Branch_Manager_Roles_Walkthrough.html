2. Business Rationale & Why We Implemented Everything

A detailed rationale explaining the commercial need and architectural choices for each capability:

1. Why Centralized RBAC Matrix without Backend Enforcement?

Brand Owners managing 97 staff across 6 salons need immediate visual clarity over who has access to customer phone numbers, invoices, and discount approvals before signing off on operational policies.

2. Why 9 Default Personas & Custom Role Clones?

Standard roles (Branch Manager, Reception, Stylist) cover 90% of daily workflows, while specialized bridal leads or colorist supervisors require custom elevated privileges without altering standard presets.

3. Why 5 Expandable Module Groups with Sticky Columns?

A 14-module × 6-permission table has 84 distinct toggle cells. Grouping them by Core, Business, Admin, Analytics, and Configuration prevents cognitive overload and keeps the matrix intuitive on desktop and tablet.

4. Why Safety Confirmation for Sensitive Permissions?

Accidentally granting Delete or Finance access to junior staff introduces compliance risk. Portaled security confirmation prompts force deliberate executive verification.

5. Why Inherited Permissions in Staff Assignment Dossier?

Staff members inherit permissions from their role. The assignment dossier makes it crystal clear what actions a specific stylist or receptionist can perform at their assigned branch.

6. Why Maker-Checker Approval Trail for Permission Deltas?

Enterprise ISO & SOC2 standards require dual-authorization for security privilege escalations. The maker-checker workflow allows branch managers to request changes and HQ CFOs to approve them.

3. UI Architecture & Page Navigation Flow

The Roles & Permissions module is structured under a unified responsive master container with persistent sub-navigation:

  • Roles Directory (/roles-permissions?tab=roles): Master system & custom roles table, user counts, Create Role modal, and Role Dossier modal.
  • Permission Matrix (/roles-permissions?tab=matrix): Role selector, 5 collapsible module groups, 6 permission types, and bulk shortcuts.
  • Role Assignments (/roles-permissions?tab=assignments): Staff credential ledger, branch scope filters, Change Role modal, and Assignment Dossier.
  • Permission History (/roles-permissions?tab=history): Immutable configuration change log, maker-checker approval actions, and Delta Dossier.
