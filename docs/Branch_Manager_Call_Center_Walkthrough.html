Executive Summary & Central Concierge Authority
The Call Center & Central Concierge Desk operates as an Admin / Head Office level multi-branch command center. Rather than individual salon branch floor staff answering phone inquiries during peak rush hours, central concierge agents handle inbound calls via WebRTC/SIP cloud telephony (Exotel/Twilio), perform instant CTI Caller ID lookups for VIP dossiers and allergy warnings, dispatch conflict-free bookings across all 5 branches (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Bhiwandi), manage 5-stage customer grievances, and drive automated lapsed client rebooking campaigns.

2. Business Rationale & Why We Implemented Everything

Operational Excellence in Luxury Salon Chains
Field-by-field and module-by-module explanation of the business impact delivered by the Central Concierge architecture:
  • Why Central Concierge Authority? In high-end salon chains, front-desk receptionists are frequently overwhelmed by walk-in welcomes, retail checkouts, and credit card POS settlements. Centralizing telephony guarantees that 100% of inbound booking calls are answered professionally within 2 rings with zero missed revenue.
  • Why CTI Screen-Pop with Chemical Allergy Alerts? Scalp contraindications (e.g., Ammonia sensitivity, nut-oil allergies) are major liability risks. Displaying allergy warnings instantly when the guest dials in ensures agents book the correct organic services and assign certified colorists.
  • Why Cross-Branch Capacity Balancing? When a guest's primary salon (e.g. Bandra West) has no open chairs at 4 PM on Saturday, the agent can immediately cross-book them at a nearby sister salon (e.g. Juhu or Worli) rather than losing the booking to a competitor.
  • Why Goodwill Wallet Compensation in Grievance Redressal? Customer retention is 5x cheaper than acquisition. Empowering call center managers to instantly credit ₹500–₹1,500 into the guest's salon wallet directly recovers dissatisfied clients before negative online reviews are posted.
  • Why Automated Lapsed Client Recovery? Guests who have not visited in 45–90 days represent untapped pipeline. Triggering personalized promotional vouchers (`REVIVE20`) drives consistent month-on-month win-back revenue.
