# Payment Service

## Responsibilities
- Payment intents with strict idempotency keys
- Booking deposits & payment links (SMS/WhatsApp pay links)
- Cash, UPI, Card, and POS terminal transactions
- Multi-gateway integration (Razorpay, Stripe)
- Payment verification & cryptographic signature checking
- Refunds management & partial refund tracking
- Gateway webhook parsing & idempotency protection
- Settlement reconciliation
