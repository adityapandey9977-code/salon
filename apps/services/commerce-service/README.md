# Commerce Service

## Responsibilities
- Service Catalogue, Categories & Branch Pricing
- Service Recipe / BOM (Bill of Materials) definitions
- Packages, Prepaid Services & Shared Family Bundles
- Memberships, Recurring Benefits & Discount Rules
- Customer Prepaid Wallet Ledger (Credit/Debit transactions)
- Customer Loyalty Points & Tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum)
- Coupons & Promotional Discounts
- POS Cart, Checkout Orchestration & GST Calculation
- Invoices & Invoice Line Items
- Domain events: `SaleCompleted`, `MembershipActivated`, `PackageRedeemed`, `LoyaltyPointsEarned`

`5006`

## Logical Database
`commerce_db`
