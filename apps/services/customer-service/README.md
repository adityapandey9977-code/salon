# Customer Service

## Responsibilities
- Customer CRM & 360 profile
- Lead management, acquisition sources & conversion tracking
- Client interaction notes & history
- Cautions & safety alerts (allergies, scalp sensitivity, chemical warnings)
- Client preferences (favorite beverages, preferred stylists, contact preferences)
- Customer tagging & segmentation
- NOTE: Packages, memberships, wallet and loyalty ledger transactional data belong to `commerce-service`.
