# Inventory Service

## Responsibilities
- Master SKU catalogue (retail products & salon consumable backbar supplies)
- Branch-level stock tracking & stock levels
- Product batches & expiry management
- Immutable stock movements ledger (every addition/deduction is strictly auditable)
- Suppliers catalogue & procurement
- Purchase Orders (POs) & Goods Received Notes (GRNs)
- Inter-branch stock transfers
- Stock adjustments, write-offs & physical stocktaking audits
- Low stock notifications & automatic reorder alerts
